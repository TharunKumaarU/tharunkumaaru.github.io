About

Build it, ship it, keep it running.

I joined Missan IT Solutions as an intern in July 2025 and converted to full time three months later. Since then I've worked across five platforms, each one from the database schema through to the production server it runs on.

The deployment side is where I differ from most people at my level. Containers on Ubuntu behind nginx for one set of clients, and Windows Server IIS builds for the on-premise ones, including installing and configuring Windream ECM in their environments.

The thing I'd point at first isn't a feature, it's a habit: every production data fix I ship goes out with a tested repair and rollback script written before I touch anything. I learned that the way most people do, on live systems where mistakes cost someone their afternoon.

I use AI-assisted development heavily. It's how one person keeps five platforms moving. The architecture calls, the schema design and the debugging are still mine.
